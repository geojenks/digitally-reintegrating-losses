Try it: mark losses and preview stitch fills

Digitally Reintegrating Losses in Heritage Embroidery · GCH 2026

Upload a photograph of an embroidery (or use the sample), select the loss regions with the box, brush or magic-wand tools, and assign each region one of three stitch types: French knot, satin or silk purl. Preview fill shows a quick procedural stand-in texture computed in your browser. The real generative fill runs in the Colab notebook, using the image and per-stitch mask files you export here. Only these three stitch types are available unless you train your own stitch LoRA. Nothing is uploaded: everything stays in your browser.
